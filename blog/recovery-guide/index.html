Planning Recovery After Cancer Surgery

A framework for thinking about mobility, nutrition, follow-up and practical support after an operation.

Before the operation

Planning begins before surgery. Understanding mobility expectations, medication instructions, nutrition, wound care and who will help at home can make the postoperative period easier to navigate.

The early recovery phase

Early recovery often focuses on safe mobilisation, symptom control, nutrition and monitoring for complications. The exact timeline varies substantially by operation and patient.

Follow-up and longer-term support

Follow-up is part of the pathway. Ask when pathology will be reviewed, how postoperative symptoms should be reported and what the next oncology or surgical appointment will involve.
